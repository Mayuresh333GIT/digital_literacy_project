**1. Use strong and unique passwords**: Ensure that your passwords contain a combination of characters, numbers, and symbols. Never use the same password for different accounts, like email, bank accounts, and social media.

**2. Use two-factor authentication**: Enable two-factor authentication for your email, cloud storage, and social media accounts.

**3. Be cautious of suspicious links**: Never click on suspicious links received on SMS, emails, and messaging apps, as they may be phishing websites that may steal personal data.

**4. Protect your personal data**: Never reveal personal data, like Aadhar number, phone number, and college ID on social media.

**5. Be cautious about UPI payment requests**: Never accept a “collect request” on UPI without confirming the person asking for the payment. Fraudsters may send fake requests pretending to be friends, sellers, or service providers.

**6. Never scan the QR code if you need to receive money**: Never scan the QR code if the person sends you a QR code to receive money.

**7. Use secure networks**: It is recommended that you do not log in to your financial accounts when connected through public Wi-Fi networks like those in a café or college campus unless they have the required security.

**8. Keep your device updated**: It is recommended to regularly update the security software on your device to prevent any cyber attacks.

**9. Report cyber fraud immediately**: If you face any cyber fraud, it is recommended that you report it on the National Cyber Crime Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or call the Cyber Crime Helpline – 1930 immediately.
